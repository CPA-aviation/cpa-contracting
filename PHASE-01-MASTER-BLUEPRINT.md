## 2. Brand positioning & creative direction

### 2.1 Strategic reality (honest positioning)

This is **not** a heritage contractor with a multi-decade archive. The digital experience must earn international credibility through **precision, discipline, and material honesty** — not through invented scale.

**Positioning statement (proposed — requires approval):**  
> C P A CONTRACTING L.L.C is a Dubai-licensed building and road contracting company. We build with clarity, controlled process, and accountable delivery — a new contractor designed for the standards of international clients operating in the UAE.

**Brand promise (proposed):** Clarity of scope. Integrity of execution. Discipline of site.

**Brand antithesis:** Template “construction company” sites, stock hard-hat heroism, inflated claims, decorative CGI skylines unrelated to actual work.

### 2.2 Audience (provisional until company confirms)

1. **Primary:** Developers, main contractors, and consultants evaluating subcontract / specialty contracting partners in Dubai
2. **Secondary:** Private clients and facility owners seeking licensed building/road works
3. **Internal/regulatory:** Banks, insurers, prequalification desks needing clear legal identity

### 2.3 Creative north star

**Theme:** *Measured Structure* — the site should feel like a well-drawn construction document: exact type, intentional margins, material textures (concrete, asphalt, steel, dust light), and motion that behaves like engineered systems (load, sequence, alignment) — never like marketing fluff.

**Art direction pillars**

1. **Singular compositions** — each viewport one idea; brand name hero-level on Home
2. **Material photography over illustration** — real site texture when assets exist; until then, restrained typographic architecture
3. **Arabic + English as equal citizens** — bilingual as structural layout, not a toggle afterthought
4. **Restraint as luxury** — fewer sections, deeper craft
5. **Honesty as aesthetic** — if projects are not ready, the Projects page becomes a curated capability framework rather than fake portfolio tiles

### 2.4 Forbidden patterns (project rules)

- Generic AI SaaS/construction templates
- Card grids as the default section language
- Purple/indigo or cream-terracotta AI clichés; glassmorphism; glow stacks
- Floating badges, stat strips of invented metrics
- Identical motion presets on every block
- Claiming affiliation with other CPA brands without written approval

## 4. Proposed visual identity

### 4.1 Naming presentation

- Primary lockup: **C P A CONTRACTING** with **L.L.C** as regulated legal suffix (smaller optical weight)
- Arabic lockup: **سي بيه ايه للمقاولات ش.ذ.م.م**
- Do not invent an expanded meaning for “C P A” on-site unless the company defines it in writing

### 4.2 Colour system (proposed — for approval)

Industrial-precision palette grounded in Dubai site light — **not** generic navy+orange construction kits.

| Token | Role | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| `ink` | Primary text | Near-black warm charcoal |
| `paper` | Page ground | Cool off-white with slight mineral cast |
| `dust` | Secondary ground | Soft concrete grey |
| `asphalt` | Dark sections | Deep graphite (not pure #000) |
| `signal` | CTA / focus | Controlled copper-oxide or safety amber used sparingly |
| `steel` | Rules / hairlines | Mid cool grey |
| `permit` | Success/verified chips | Muted institutional green (rare) |

Avoid: purple gradients, neon cyan, glossy gold luxury clichés.

### 4.3 Typography (proposed)

| Role | Direction |
|---|---|
| Display EN | Contemporary grotesque with architectural sharpness (e.g. **Neue Haas Grotesk / ABC Diatype / similar licensed**) — not Inter/Roboto/Arial |
| Display AR | High-quality Arabic companion (e.g. **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** or premium paired Arabic) with optical size care |
| Body | Readable grotesque; generous measure on desktop, disciplined line-length |
| Mono / meta | Tabular figures for license numbers, dates, TRN on About (document aesthetic) |

### 4.4 Imagery rules

- Prefer: structural detail, road beds, formwork, rebar rhythm, site dawn/dusk, bilingual signage discipline
- Avoid: handshake stock, generic skyline CGI, hard-hat selfie culture, unrelated megaproject photography
- Until real assets exist: typographic/material UI — **no random Unsplash construction filler**

### 4.5 UI geometry

- Large margins; asymmetric but grid-true layouts
- Sharp or micro-radius only where interaction requires
- Rules and baselines instead of card chrome
- Cards reserved for interactive modules (forms, filters) only

## 5. Motion design strategy

### 5.1 Motion language: “Load → Align → Settle”

Motion should feel like surveying and setting out a site:

1. **Load** — elements arrive with weight (slight vertical bias, opacity)
2. **Align** — lines, type, and media snap to shared axes
3. **Settle** — ease-out to rest; no endless floating

### 5.2 GSAP architecture (when implementation begins)

| Layer | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| `gsap-core` | Tweens, defaults, `matchMedia`, reduced-motion forks |
| `gsap-timeline` | Section choreography; Home intro sequence |
| `gsap-scrolltrigger` | Chapter pinning on Approach; scrubbed media reveals; Home story |
| `gsap-react` | `useGSAP` + context cleanup in Next/React |
| `gsap-performance` | Transform/opacity preference; avoid layout thrash |
| Plugins (selective) | ScrollTrigger first; SplitText only if licensed & needed; Flip for shared-element page transitions; **no ScrollSmoother unless performance budget proven** |

### 5.3 Page-specific motion (planned, not built)

| Surface | Motion |
|---|---|
| Home hero | Brand wordmark materialises; single headline; one CTA; full-bleed media crossfade — **no overlay chips** |
| Home services | Two activity names reveal via alignment, not card flip |
| Approach | Pinned vertical stages with scrubbed progress indicator |
| Projects | If content exists: directional wipe / mask tied to scroll; if empty: quiet static honesty |
| Page transitions | Shared axis wipe or clip-path; keep under ~500ms |
| Contact | Minimal motion; form focus states over spectacle |

### 5.4 Three.js / WebGL decision

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Full WebGL site | **No** for Phase 02–03 — unjustified for a two-service contractor with limited assets |
| Targeted Three.js moment | **Optional later** — e.g. abstract terrain/road ribbon on Home **only if** it passes performance + a11y and does not replace real photography |
| SVG / Canvas | **Yes, selectively** — route alignment diagrams, section markers, progress glyphs on Approach |
| Video | Preferred over WebGL for cinematic feel when real footage exists |

### 5.5 Accessibility & reduced motion

- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → instant opacity fades or static states; disable scrub pin complexities
- Focus-visible rings using `signal` token
- Do not convey unique information by motion alone
- Respect bilingual screen-reader order (document `lang` switching strategy)
